# Keyword strategy (evidence-based, no invented metrics)

## No proprietary data
Search volume, difficulty and ranking are not available here. Never state numbers for them. Use qualitative estimates and label them `[inferência]` or `[hipótese]`.

## Where candidate terms come from (cite each)
- The app's own page and what it truly does `[página]`/`[usuário]`.
- Titles and descriptions of verified competitors `[página]`/`[web]`.
- Public search results for the phrases users would type `[web]` (what ranks, how results are framed).
- Language users use in reviews `[página]`.
- Wording of search suggestions if a tool exposes them `[web]`.

## Classification
- **Primary**: describes the core function; high relevance; goes in title/short description.
- **Secondary**: key features, user-type or scenario terms.
- **Long-tail**: specific intent phrases; good for full description.
- **Synonyms/related**: alternative wording of the same intent.

Intent types: informational, task/solution ("how do I…"), category browse, brand/competitor (do not use competitor brand names in metadata).

## Prioritization
Score each term qualitatively (Alta/Média/Baixa): relevance to real functionality (gate: low relevance = discard, regardless of popularity), intent fit with the target user, competitive saturation seen in competitors' metadata, differentiation potential. Reject terms the app cannot truthfully serve.
